## Full description (4000)

```
Photograph a toy. In seconds you get a straight answer about it.

DollChecker looks at the toy the way an experienced early-years specialist would
— and then keeps working long after the scan.

WHAT A SCAN TELLS YOU
• Safety — choking risks, small parts, magnets, batteries, sharp edges, cords,
  loud sound, questionable materials. Rated red, yellow or green, with the
  reasoning spelled out.
• The right age — whether it fits the child you're buying for, not just the box.
• What it develops — around 20 skills, from fine motor to language to
  problem-solving, scored individually.
• How to play with it — concrete activities, not "encourage exploration".

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE SCAN
• Your toy collection, built automatically. Scan the same toy twice and it stays
  one entry.
• A development picture for each child: where their toys are strong, where the
  gaps are, and which skills nothing you own is touching.
• Three daily play missions per child — drawn from the toys already in the house
  and chosen for the skills that need the practice. Keep a streak going.
• Toy rotation: the app remembers what hasn't been played with for weeks and
  tells you to bring it back out. It usually works better than buying something.
• A parents panel: your whole household's week on one screen, plus a safety
  review of the toys you own that came back red or yellow.

BUILT FOR PARENTS WHO ARE BUSY
Every child gets their own profile. Advice is tailored to their age. Switch
between children from any screen. Available in English and Russian.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The free plan includes 10 scans a month — enough to check the shelf before a
birthday. Premium removes the limit and unlocks the toy chat, where you can ask
follow-up questions about anything you've scanned: "she's ignoring it, now
what?", "is this noise level a problem?", "how do I get more out of it?"

HONEST ABOUT WHAT THIS IS
DollChecker is AI guidance from a photograph. It cannot open a toy, test a
material, or check a recall database. It does not replace packaging warnings,
certification marks, or your own judgement — and it will tell you so. A green
verdict means nothing obviously hazardous was visible, not that a toy is
certified safe. Always supervise young children.

Your photos stay private to your account. Delete your account from inside the
app at any time and everything goes with it.
```

## Screenshots — required, and the one thing that must be captured by hand

Both stores require real screenshots and they cannot be generated from tests
(widget tests render text without real fonts). Run the app on a device or
emulator with a seeded account and capture these six, in this order:

1. **Result screen** — a green scan with the safety badge and scores visible
2. **Result screen, scrolled** — development skills
3. **Today** — three missions and a live streak
4. **Development dashboard** — the radar with real data
5. **Collection** — a grid with 6+ toys
6. **Parents panel** — the household week plus a safety review with one red toy

Sizes: **Play** — 1080×1920 or larger, 2–8 images. **Apple** — 6.7" (1290×2796)
and 6.5" (1242×2688) sets, 3–10 images each.
