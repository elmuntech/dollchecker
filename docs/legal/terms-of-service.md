## 1. What the service is

intelligence and returns: an identification, an age recommendation, a safety
assessment, development scores, an educational score, and play ideas. It also
keeps a collection of your toys, a development dashboard, daily play missions,
and — on the paid plan — a conversation about a toy you have scanned.

## 4. AI guidance — the important part

**DollChecker gives AI-generated guidance. It is not a safety certification, an
inspection, or professional advice, and you must not treat it as one.**

Specifically:

- The analysis comes from a **photograph**. It cannot see inside a toy, test a
  material, weigh a part, measure a magnet, or know whether the item has been
  recalled.
- It is **not** a substitute for the manufacturer's age grading, the packaging
  warnings, official certification marks (CE, UKCA, ASTM F963, EN 71), or
  national recall databases. Those remain authoritative; where they disagree
  with the app, they are right and the app is wrong.
- A "green" verdict is **not** a statement that a toy is safe. It means nothing
  obviously hazardous was visible in one photograph.
- Development and educational scores are indicative, not measurements, and are
  not a developmental assessment of your child.
- Nothing in the app is medical advice. For any concern about your child's
  health or development, ask a doctor.

**You remain responsible for supervising your child and for deciding what they
play with.** Always check the packaging and the manufacturer's guidance
yourself.
