# DollChecker — Refund Policy

**Last updated: 3 August 2026**

> **Before publishing:** replace `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`s. Polar requires a published
> refund policy before an organization can sell, and both app stores ask for one
> when a subscription is involved.

Payments are processed by **Polar Software Inc.**, who are the merchant of
record for every DollChecker purchase. Refunds are issued by them, on our
instruction.

---

## Cancelling

Cancel any time from **Parents panel → Account → Manage subscription**, which
opens the billing portal. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep premium
access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Nothing is
pro-rated for a period already begun, unless one of the cases below applies.

## 14-day right of withdrawal (UK / EU)

If you are a consumer in the UK or EEA you may withdraw from the purchase within
**14 days** and receive a full refund. Because the subscription starts
immediately, you are asked to consent to that at checkout, which can reduce this
right — but as a matter of policy we honour the full 14 days on a **first**
subscription regardless.

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the address you paid with.

## Beyond 14 days

We will refund the current period if:

- the service was unavailable for a substantial part of it;
- you were charged after cancelling;
- you were charged twice; or
- a paid feature was withdrawn during a period you had paid for.

We generally will not refund simply because a subscription went unused, or
because the analysis did not agree with your own judgement of a toy — the app
gives AI guidance, and its limits are set out in the
[Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## How to ask

Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** from the address on the account, with the date of
the charge. We reply within **5 working days**. An approved refund is returned by
Polar to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 working days
depending on your bank.

## Bought through an app store

If you ever buy a subscription through Apple's In-App Purchase or Google Play
Billing rather than our checkout, that store handles the refund under its own
policy, and we cannot issue it for them:

- Apple — [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Google Play — [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
